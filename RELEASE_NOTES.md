Google App PrivApp: the latest Google app as a privileged system app + Google as the default assistant (Circle to Search, content suggestions).

- **online.zip**: downloads the newest Google app while flashing (with progress), checks its SHA-256.
- **offline.zip**: ships the Google app version in its name; no internet needed, update later from the Play Store.

Flash in Magisk / KernelSU / APatch, reboot. arm64, Android 11+. See the README for details.
